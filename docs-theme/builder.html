Template builder

Paste the cards you already have - copies of one card that differ by an entity, a name, an icon - and this works out the template they are copies of, and what each card becomes once it uses it. Nothing leaves your browser.

Paste them the way the card editor or the raw configuration editor shows them. A list of cards, a whole view or a whole dashboard all work, and cards inside stacks are found too.

To see what a single placeholder gives back, step by step, use Try a placeholder.
